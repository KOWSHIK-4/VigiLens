# VigiLens Operator Handover Checklist

## System overview
- Identify deployed application and service versions.
- List the environments and their owners.
- Record the approved architecture and service dependencies.
- Identify camera sources and their responsible contacts.

## Daily operations
- Explain where service health and error signals are reviewed.
- Document how camera disconnections and stale feeds are identified.
- Describe how detection events and notification failures are investigated.
- Identify backup status and escalation procedures.

## Access and security
- Transfer access through approved account-management processes.
- Confirm role assignments and remove access that is no longer required.
- Never share passwords, API keys, or tokens in handover documents.
- Identify where secrets are managed without copying their values.

## Open work
- List known defects, operational risks, and pending changes.
- Assign an owner and target review date for each item.
- Include links to relevant runbooks and release evidence.

The handover is complete only when the receiving operator confirms access and understands the escalation path. Do not include sensitive camera imagery or credentials in the handover.
