# Camera Frame Quality Review Guide

## Purpose
Review whether camera frames supplied to VigiLens are suitable for the configured detection workflow.

## Quality dimensions
- **Resolution:** Confirm the source resolution meets the detector's documented input requirements.
- **Blur:** Check for motion blur or focus problems that obscure relevant objects.
- **Lighting:** Review low-light, glare, and strong backlight conditions.
- **Occlusion:** Identify whether important regions are frequently blocked.
- **Stability:** Check for unexpected rotation, framing changes, or intermittent frozen frames.

## Validation approach
- Use authorized test footage representing expected operating conditions.
- Record source configuration and detector version.
- Compare representative clear and degraded frames.
- Check whether low-quality input is surfaced or handled safely.
- Avoid interpreting poor-quality frames as confirmed absence of an event.

## Reporting
Record observations, example timestamps, camera identifier, and corrective actions. Avoid storing or sharing personal imagery beyond approved retention and access rules.
